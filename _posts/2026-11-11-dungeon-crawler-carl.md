## The Book

**Title:** _Dungeon Crawler Carl_
**Author:** Matt Dinniman
**Finished:** November 3, 2026
**Rating:** ★★★½

## One-paragraph summary

It's a Literature RPG (LitRPG), science fiction, fantsy type of book. Reading it is like reading a comedic version of Diablo (the Blizzard game). It's about a guy named Carl and his ex-girlfriend's cat, Donut. The world ends and they are dragged into a dungeon crawl intergalatic television game show. They and the other humans who are also part of the dungeon crawl must descend the dungeon and "level up" by beating the monsters set by the alien overlords.

## What stood out to me

- Kinda felt like the book was written by AI
- There's quite a lot of other books in this series and it can get repetitive and confusing.
- Too many words for me to read but it's enjoyable

## Who should read it

Anyone that is into dungeons and dragons with a mix of sci-fi thrown into it. Maybe if you played Diablo 3 or similar games. It's got a cat so there's some cat jokes there.
