## The Book

**Title:** _Theo of Golden_
**Author:** Allen Levi
**Finished:** November 3, 2026
**Rating:** ★★★★

## One-paragraph summary

Theo of Golden is about a mysterious old man who visits the town of Golden. There he visits a cafe and sees lots of portriats done by a local artist. Theo decides to gift these portriats to the subjects themselves and in the process he builds meaningful relationships with the town's inhabitants. Throughout this story, more and more of Theo is revealed in his conversations with the local residents.

## What stood out to me

- Generiosity, kindness and taking the time to know others is important.
- You never know the reasons for someone else's actions unless you take time to find out.

## Favorite quote

## Who should read it

If you like a feel-good story this one is pretty good. It's similar to a Man called Ove. Kind of a sappy book but I enjoyed it but then again I'm a sentimental.
