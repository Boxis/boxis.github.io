## The Book

**Title:** _Digital Minimalism_
**Author:** Cal Newport
**Finished:** July 2026
**Rating:** ★★★★☆

## One-paragraph summary

Actionable plan on how to reduce screen-time whether it's mobile or PC. Main idea is to do a 30 day digital declutter which aims to cut out ALL non-essential digital uses (youtube, reddit, IG, tiktok, etc.) as long as it doesn't negatively affect career or life.

## Key takeaways

Digital Minimalism

A philosophy of technology use in which you focus your online time on a small number of carefully selected and optimized activities that strongly support things you value, and then happily miss out on everything else.

Do a _Digital Declutter_

- Thirty-day period where you take a break from optional technologies in your life.
- Rediscover activities and behaviors that you find satisfying and meaningful.
- At the end, reintroduce optional tech, starting from blank state. Determine the value for each tech reintroduced and how to use it to maximize this value.

### Tips for Digital Declutter

#### Step 1: Define Technology Rules
What's "Optional" Technology?
- Apps, websites, and related digital tools that are delivered through a computer screen or phone
- Meant to entertain, inform, or connect you
- E.g., text messaging, Instagram, Reddit
- Microwave, radio, electric toothbrush are NOT
- Video games are on the border

#### Step 2: Take a Thirty-Day Break
Aggressively explore higher-quality activities to fill in the time left vacant by optional tech.

This period should be one of strenuous activity and experimentation.

#### Step 3: Reintroduce Technology
Apply The Minimalist Technology Screen

1. Serve something you deeply value (offering some benefit is not enough).
2. Be the best way to use technology to serve this value (if it's not, replace it with something better).
3. Have a role in your life that is constrained with a standard operating procedure that specifies when and how you use it.

### Practices

#### Spend Time Alone
- Leave your phone at home
- Take Long Walks
- Write letters to yourself

#### Don't Click Like
- Consolidate texting
- Hold conversation office hours

#### Reclaim Leisure
- Prioritize demanding activity over passive consumption
- Use skills to produce valuable things in the physical world
- Seek activities that require real-world, structured social interactions
- Fix or build something every week
- Schedule your low-quality leisure
- Join something
- Follow leisure plans
    - Seasonal Leisure Plan
    - Weekly Leisure Plan

## Who should read it

Anyone looking to reduce screen-time or mindless scrolling.
